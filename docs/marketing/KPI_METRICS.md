# BK01 Marketing & Commercial KPI Metrics

**Status:** LOCKED DEFINITIONS — thresholds require pilot evidence

This document uses the canonical event/metric definitions in `../07_ANALYTICS_KPI_SPEC.md`. It must not redefine activation or first value.

## Acquisition
- qualified leads/visits by source;
- signup-start rate from qualified landing sessions;
- cost per qualified signup when paid acquisition begins.

## Activation and value
- onboarding completion rate;
- median time from signup to booking link published;
- activation rate = trial shops reaching first confirmed real customer booking after setup;
- first-value rate = trial shops that handle a real booking successfully without WSTERA repair;
- median time to first value.

## Commercial
- trial-to-paid conversion;
- final price acceptance/WTP distribution during pilot;
- Basic/Pro mix;
- MRR and paid-shop cohort retention;
- logo churn and cancellation reasons;
- variable automation cost and support burden per paid shop.

## Product proof for messaging
- booking completion/cancellation/no-show distributions;
- deposit adoption and verification outcomes;
- notification delivery success;
- merchant-reported manual follow-up time before/after, only when measurement method is recorded.

## Paid acquisition readiness gate
Do not scale ads until onboarding completion, first value, trial-to-paid, early retention and support burden have enough pilot observations to estimate a viable funnel. Signup volume alone is never a readiness signal.
