# BK01 Launch Plan

**Status:** LOCKED PLAN — public launch not authorized

## Stage 0 — BK-A contract closure
No external launch. Close all V1 Required gaps and pass technical gates.

## Stage 1 — Controlled pilot
Recruit 5–15 primary-ICP shops. Use real business workflows with explicit consent/readiness. Instrument onboarding, first value, bookings, deposits, notifications, support burden and WTP. Any P0/P1 security/money/booking-integrity incident pauses cohort expansion.

## Stage 2 — Commercial lock
Refresh competitor prices/features; review unit cost; approve final Basic/Pro price, Pro auto-slip allowance and any managed-LINE allowance; approve launch claims from pilot evidence; complete legal/privacy checklist.

## Stage 3 — Limited paid launch
Open paid acquisition only to primary ICP. Keep onboarding/support capacity capped. Monitor activation, first-value time, support load, webhook/provider failures, churn signals and booking integrity daily during initial cohort.

## Stage 4 — Public V1
Requires all release gates PASS, final pricing owner approval, production runbooks, legal/privacy readiness and independent review PASS.

## Launch artifacts
- landing page with evidence-approved claims only;
- pricing page matching `04_PRICING_ENTITLEMENTS.md` exactly;
- onboarding/help content for shop setup, LINE, deposits and booking policy;
- privacy/terms/support/contact surfaces;
- release evidence bundle and incident contacts.

## Rollback/stop rule
Marketing is paused before technical rollback is debated when there is suspected cross-tenant exposure, deposit-slip exposure, booking corruption/collision, billing entitlement drift or material misleading product claim.
