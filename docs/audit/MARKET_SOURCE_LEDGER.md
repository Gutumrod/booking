## Interpretation and refresh rules
- Prices, quotas, trials, taxes, fees and feature availability are time-sensitive and must be rechecked before final commercial lock.
- “Unlimited” is recorded only when the vendor says so; it does not imply absence of fair-use/rate limits hidden in terms.
- Public absence of a feature is `UNKNOWN`, not `NO`, unless an official comparison explicitly excludes it.
- Marketplace/transaction fees are separated from subscription price where the official source exposes them.
- Vendor security, compliance, support-speed, adoption and outcome statements are not independently verified BK01 evidence.
- Global price pages may render by locale/currency; do not convert or compare raw price without a dated FX/tax normalization exercise.
- NSO/OSMEP counts are market-universe indicators only. No TAM/SAM/SOM is claimed from them without addressability and willingness-to-pay filters.
- PromptPay scale supports Thailand-native payment relevance only; it does not prove deposits reduce no-show.

**Refresh trigger:** before final public price lock, before launch-message lock, after a material competitor/package change, or 60 days after 2026-08-28 — whichever occurs first.
